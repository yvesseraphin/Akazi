## 2. Market Problem & Strategic Positioning

### 2.1 The German Challenge: Structural Skilled Labor Shortage (*Fachkräftemangel*)
- Declining applicant numbers and hundreds of thousands of unfilled apprenticeship positions across Germany annually.
- Rapidly aging population and generational demographic shifts.
- Growing operational pressure on German employers to secure loyal, disciplined, and trainable entry-level talent.

### 2.2 The Rwandan Opportunity: A Young, Forward-Looking Talent Pool
- Approximately 60% of Rwanda’s population is under 25 years old.
- Young individuals actively pursue vocational education and career progression with long-term ambition.
- Stable, safe, clean, and well-organized country in East Africa with advanced digital and civic infrastructure.

### 2.3 The Differentiator: Targeted Migration into Vocational Training vs. Uncontrolled Migration
- **Core Statement:** *"Not uncontrolled migration, but targeted immigration into vocational training. Our participants do not come from a traumatized refugee situation; they make a conscious, structured decision for an apprenticeship and long-term professional development in Germany."*
- Candidates make an intentional, structured, and legally compliant commitment to building their future in Germany.

1. **Young, Motivated Population:** 
   One of the youngest demographics globally (60% under 25). High eagerness to acquire advanced practical skills and take on professional responsibility.

4. **Fast Language Acquisition & Learning Agility:**

5. **Political & Societal Stability:** 
   Rwanda is one of the safest and most efficiently governed nations in Africa, facilitating orderly consular procedures, criminal background vetting, and document verification.
6. **International Connectivity:** 
   A burgeoning economic, technology, and transport hub in East Africa, bolstered by state-of-the-art infrastructure and the new international airport near Kigali.

## 5. Language Preparation & Communication Concept

- **Mandatory Pre-Departure Standard:** German Language Level **B2** (certified through recognized test providers such as Goethe-Institut or telc).
- **Core Philosophy:** Passing the B2 exam is merely the baseline prerequisite.
- **Training Focus:** Practical, real-world communicative competence for everyday work environments:
  - Comprehending operational instructions and safety protocols on the job.
  - Asking questions confidently whenever ambiguity arises.
  - Articulating ideas clearly to colleagues, supervisors, and vocational teachers.
  - Mastering trade-specific technical vocabulary for the vocational school (*Berufsschule*).

## 6. The 5-Step Operational Process ("Our Process / Our Service")

### Step 01: Needs Assessment (*Bedarf klären*)
- Defining the target apprenticeship trades (e.g., mechatronics, HVAC, industrial mechanics, hospitality, nursing, logistics) and required headcount with the employer.
- Establishing an exact profile of requirements (physical capabilities, shift schedules, educational prerequisites).

### Step 02: Candidate Selection & Language Academy (*Auswahl & Sprachschule*)
- Comprehensive on-the-ground talent sourcing and vetting in Rwanda.
- Intensive German language immersion up to certified B2 level.
- Cultural onboarding covering workplace etiquette, civic life, and daily routines in Germany.

### Step 03: Precision Matching & Employment Contract (*Matching*)
- Tailored candidate pre-selection matched against company profiles.
- Facilitating structured virtual video interviews between employers and pre-screened candidates.
- Employer makes the final hiring decision and executes the official German vocational training contract (*Berufsausbildungsvertrag*).

### Step 04: Immigration, Visa & Arrival (*Einreise & Ankunft*)
- Complete management of the visa procedure under **§ 16a AufenthG** (Residence Act for Vocational Training).
- Coordination with the Federal Employment Agency (Zentralstelle für Auslands- und Fachvermittlung – ZAV) and the German Embassy in Kigali.
- Logistics management (flight booking, airport pickup, travel insurance) and structured welcome onboarding.

### Step 05: Continuous Day-to-Day Accompaniment (*Begleitung im Alltag*)
- Hands-on support with local registration (*Einwohnermeldeamt*), bank account setup, statutory health insurance, and lease agreements.
- Designated personal point of contact for both the employer and apprentice to resolve personal, operational, or academic questions.
- Ongoing group meetings, mentorship check-ins, and mediation until the final exam (IHK / HWK Abschlussprüfung).

## 7. Legal & Regulatory Framework

- **Immigration Statute:** Legal entry and residency under **§ 16a Aufenthaltsgesetz (AufenthG)** (residence permit for vocational and advanced vocational training).
- **Agency Approval:** Fast-track approval procedures via the German Federal Employment Agency (Bundesagentur für Arbeit / ZAV).
- **Educational Standards:** Direct adherence to the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR / GER) and certified Goethe-Institut criteria.
- **Risk Mitigation:** Clear allocation of operational responsibilities between Akazi, the employer, and the apprentice to protect employer investment.

## 13. Expanded Service Details from the Product Brief

The following details extend the company narrative above. They describe Akazi's stated service model; check current public copy or confirm with the company before presenting them as contractual guarantees.

### 13.1 Candidate Matching
Matching is intended to consider the company environment, occupational requirements, candidate strengths and personality, and regional preferences. Employers interview prepared candidates and make the hiring decision; Akazi coordinates the process. Avoid claiming Akazi alone decides the placement.

### 13.2 Ongoing Apprentice Support
Akazi's accompaniment continues through the apprenticeship (typically two to three years, depending on the occupation) and aims to support completion of the chamber examination. The stated support includes:
- A consistent personal contact for the apprentice and employer.
- Regular group meetings and check-ins to identify concerns early.
- Help with housing and establishing stable living conditions.
- Support navigating vocational school, language challenges, and academic or relationship difficulties.
- Early mediation for workplace conflict, personal crises, or performance concerns.
- Attention to personal security and mental wellbeing during relocation and integration.

Support should be described as guidance and accompaniment, not a guarantee of housing, mental-health treatment, exam success, or a specific outcome unless the company confirms that commitment.

### 13.3 Commercial Model: Treat Unconfirmed Details Carefully
The product brief indicates that German companies pay for the end-to-end service and refers to a risk model. It does not establish fees, replacement terms, refunds, or a placement guarantee. Do not invent or promise these terms in website copy. The webinar is described as a free, non-binding company information session; verify dates and booking availability before publishing a schedule.

### 13.4 Positioning Summary
Akazi presents itself as a full-service partner for international vocational training: candidate sourcing and selection in Rwanda, B2 German preparation with workplace communication, matching, visa and arrival coordination, and continued support for both apprentice and employer. The distinction is sustained accompaniment beyond arrival, rather than a placement ending when the apprentice starts work. Keep the legal distinction clear: the route described is vocational training under § 16a AufenthG, not asylum or a general work visa.
